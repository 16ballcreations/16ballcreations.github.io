Google and AI

When someone asks ChatGPT about your business

To recommend a business, Google and AI need to be sure who it is. And they're only sure when everything they find about it says the same thing.

01

People don't just search: they ask

More and more people ask ChatGPT or Google “where's a good…?” instead of scrolling through a list of results.

02

AI names only a few

It recommends the businesses it recognizes as one: the same name, the same thing they do and the same place on their website, their Google profile and their social media.

03

That's brand alignment

It's what we do: make your sign, your Instagram, your website and your Google profile tell a single story. We don't promise an AI will name you; we make sure it can understand you.

The rules of the table

Why pool?

Pool is a game of precision: every shot is worked out before it's taken. A brand is the same: every piece you publish says something, and they all have to say the same thing.

Read the table

Before every shot you read the table. Before designing, we understand your brand: where it comes from and where it's going.

Safety play

Playing safe means leaving nothing to chance. We think about what could go wrong so it doesn't happen to your customers.

Every shot counts

Every piece is a shot: the logo, the website, a post. We check everything before handing it over, so none of them is out of tune.

Questions

What people ask us

If your question isn't here, write to us: we reply within 24 hours.

Can my business show up when someone asks ChatGPT?

Nobody can promise that an AI will name you. What we can do is give it something clear to understand: your name, what you do and where you are, said the same way on your website, your Google profile and your social media. Google and AI tools recommend businesses they recognize as one, and that's exactly what aligning your brand does.

What does my brand have to do with showing up on Google?

A lot. Google doesn't just read your website: it compares your name, address, hours and what you say on your Google profile, your social media and your site. When everything tells the same story, it recognizes you as one business; when things contradict each other, it hesitates. Aligning your brand is what gives it that certainty.

Do you charge for SEO every month?

No. Every project has a fixed price. If you'd like us to keep looking after it afterwards, there's an optional care plan.

Will I depend on you for everything?

No, and that's the point. Everything we make is in your name: the domain, the accounts, the files and the copy. We teach you how to run it; if you'd rather we handle something, we do, but always with the information in your hands, so you're in control of your business.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need. There are template sites that cost very little and custom-designed sites that cost several times more. Ours are custom, built from your brand. After your MIRA and the call, we send you the exact price in writing, with no commitment.
