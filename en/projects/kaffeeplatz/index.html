KaffeePlatz, a store that feels like a good cup

A coffee-gear store where you don't pick a device: you pick how you like your coffee, and the brand walks you all the way to the order.

kaffeeplatz.co ↗ Start your free MIRA

Where it started

Where it came from

KaffeePlatz sold through a template store. It worked, but it didn't look like the brand: it looked like any other, and the visuals weren't up to the care the owner puts into every product.

And sales didn't close in a cart: they closed in a conversation. Every customer ended up on WhatsApp, asking what was right for them.

The decisions

Why we did it this way

  1. Four proposals with the same content

    To make the comparison fair, we built four visual directions with the same structure and the same copy. That way the choice was about the brand, not about which one had more content.

  2. Black, gold and linen

    The chosen direction lives on black paper, with gold and a strip of linen. The texture isn't decoration: it's part of the identity, like the packaging of good coffee.

  3. One button that shines

    Each screen has a single filled gold piece: the main action. At 10:1 contrast it reads clearly and guides without shouting.

  4. The order ends on WhatsApp

    Since the sale was already a conversation, we respected it: every product opens WhatsApp with a ready message naming the variant. Nothing gets lost between the page and the chat.

  5. You pick a taste, not a device

    The "How do you like your coffee?" finder starts from your taste. Each recommendation quotes, word for word, the brand's article behind it: nothing invented.

  6. Nothing breaks in the move

    Every address of the old store leads to its match in the new one, so nobody arriving from an old link or from Google gets lost.

The work

What we did

  • An online store with a catalog and filters you can share on WhatsApp
  • The finder that recommends by how you like your coffee
  • Direct orders on WhatsApp, with the message written for you
  • "Notify me" on sold-out products
  • A brand guide with colors, typefaces and checked contrast rules
  • Image and social media advice, so the store and the profiles tell the same story

Services: Online stores · Websites · Image and social media advisory

Today

What changed

The store has been live since late September 2026. Today KaffeePlatz looks like what it is: a brand that knows coffee and helps you choose.

The sale is still a conversation, but now it starts on a page that has already done half the work.
