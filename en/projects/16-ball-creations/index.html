Where it started

Where it came from

For years, 16 Ball Creations told too many stories at once: video, software, tutorials. We did everything, and that's why it was hard to understand what we did.

It was exactly the problem we help our clients solve: a brand that tells a different story depending on where you look.

The decisions

Why we did it this way

  1. Back to the sixteenth ball

    A pool table has fifteen balls. The sixteenth doesn't exist, so we invented it. That's where the name and the promise come from: if it doesn't exist, we create it.

  2. A table you can play

    The home page is a real pool table: you shoot, and the first ball you hit tints the site. We don't tell you we're creative: we let you play.

  3. Look first, offer later

    Everything starts with the MIRA: before offering you anything, we look at your brand. Every proposal starts from your story, not from a package.

  4. One promise

    We stopped offering everything to promise one thing: that your business tells a single story on its sign, Instagram, website, Google and AI.

  5. Our own typefaces, served by us

    The site's fonts live on the site itself: it loads faster, depends on no one, and looks the same in the documents we send.

  6. We use what we promise

    Every document we send follows the same guide, and we track every MIRA and every call in our own panel. We practice the order we sell.
