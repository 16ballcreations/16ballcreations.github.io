Where it started

Where it came from

PsicoFormando started with nothing but a name and a deep wish to walk alongside families. There was knowledge and a warm voice, but no way for people to recognize her.

Over the years came the videos, the social media and a WordPress site that, in time, stopped being looked after. The brand had grown, but its home online had fallen behind.

The decisions

Why we did it this way

  1. The shapes instead of a repeated logo

    The brand has four shapes: a circle, a triangle, a square and a piece. We made them the language of the whole site, because they say what the logo says without repeating it everywhere.

  2. Flat colors, no shadows or gradients

    A website for families has to feel calm. Matte colors and wide corners lower the noise, and flat shapes stay readable even when small.

  3. The same world as her photos

    The blue comes from the logo; the pastels, from the mural behind her in her videos. The site and the photos feel like one place.

  4. No stock photos, no photos of children

    It's a brand built on trust. So there are no catalog images and no photos of children: only her, her space and her work.

  5. Educate, never promise

    The content is educational, not therapy, and it never promises results. On sensitive topics, the site points to professional help.

  6. So she can look after it herself

    We left her a guide to maintain the site with an AI assistant. In a blind test, the assistant refused to write a promise of results and stopped a change that broke the design.

The work

What we did

  • Brand identity: logo, colors, shapes and visual style
  • Recording and editing her videos
  • Running and organizing her social media
  • A new website, with 76 articles and 16 pages brought over from the old one without losing a single address
  • Signup for each program, handing off to WhatsApp to arrange payment
  • A testimonial form, surveys and a private panel to read the answers

Services: Brand identity · Websites

Today

What changed

Today PsicoFormando is an outreach project with over 18,000 followers and an identity people recognize in every video, every post and every page.

Her website is her home again: it sells her programs, collects testimonials, and she can maintain it herself, without depending on anyone.
