City Roads Wear, the city on your back

A Medellín brand that prints the map of your city on hoodies and T-shirts, with a style, a video and a store that feel like the street.

Where it started

Where it came from

City Roads Wear had something you can't buy: an idea with roots. Wearing the streets of your city on your back, so wherever you go, people can tell where you're from.

The clothes already existed, in heavy cotton and screen print. For its relaunch it needed a look that lived up to what people wear, and a store of its own to show them in.

The decisions

Why we did it this way

  1. Pasted on the street

    The style comes from a city wall: a huge map pasted at an angle, the name sprayed in graffiti, photocopied cutouts, taped-up paper. Everything you see looks like it's been outside.

  2. The data names the city, not the color

    Each city is recognized by its code and its coordinates, like a sign. That way no color gets tied to a city, and every collection can use its own.

  3. The whole person, with an echo

    Whoever wears the piece appears head to toe, with outlines that draw themselves and colored echoes behind. The clothes are seen worn and moving, not on a hanger.

  4. A wall instead of a catalog

    With only a few pieces, a grid looks empty. A wall with a rack and cubbies shows them all at once, like a neighborhood shop, and you swipe through it on your phone.

  5. A box that travels

    The video follows a box from Medellín to Bogotá: the trip in black and white with hand-drawn strokes, and the color comes back when it reaches the hands of whoever bought it.

  6. Paying where people already trust

    Payment happens on Mercado Libre, which people already know. The store does the falling in love; the payment stays somewhere safe and familiar.

The work

What we did

  • The «Pasted on the street» style sheet, with its six layers
  • A kit of hand-drawn effects, in seven colors, for its videos and social media
  • The vertical MDE → BOG video for the launch
  • A content plan for the relaunch
  • An online store with the wall, a page for every piece and a pre-sale sign-up
  • A panel to change products, photos and text without depending on anyone

Today

What changed

City Roads Wear relaunches in December with a face people recognize in the store, in the video and in every post.

The most important thing didn't change: the city is still on your back. Now the brand also looks the way it feels to wear it.
