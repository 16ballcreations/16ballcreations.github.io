Rivas Seven, from grief to light

A singer-songwriter from Medellín with roots in Chocó, and a website that makes the same journey as his music: from night to dawn.

Where it started

Where it came from

Rivas Seven had the music, the story and the voice. What he didn't have was a brand to tell them: he looked different on every platform, and his name changed order from one network to the next.

He needed a calling card to push his career: something a producer, a journalist or a fan could open and understand at once who he is.

The decisions

Why we did it this way

  1. From grief to light

    His music is about heartbreak with the hope of getting over it. So the page starts at night, in violet, and reaches dawn in yellow as you scroll: it makes the same journey as his songs.

  2. A sky that stays still

    The background is a fixed sky that changes color as you go. It carries the journey without pulling attention away from the story.

  3. A palette with roots

    Night violet, mango yellow, and a teal we kept as a nod to the palette he already used. The new doesn't erase what was already his.

  4. Any photo fits the story

    Photos turn black and white and are tinted from violet to mango. That way any image, old or new, fits in the same world.

  5. Magazine-cover references

    The discipline and huge type of a cover, and the golden warmth of the seventies. That's where the mix of strength and closeness comes from.

  6. Told in the first person

    The site speaks like him, telling his story, in Spanish, Portuguese and English, to reach beyond Medellín.

The work

What we did

  • Visual identity and brand colors
  • Tidying up his profiles so every network tells the same story
  • Image advice, with a lighting guide for his next photo session
  • A website in three languages, with his story, his music and a booking form

Services: Brand identity · Image and social media advisory · Websites

Today

What changed

Today Rivas Seven has a home of his own at rivasseven.com: a page that feels like listening to his songs, from night to dawn.

And he has something more useful than a pretty page: one story to tell on every network and in every conversation with whoever wants to work with him.
