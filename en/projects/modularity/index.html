Modularity, a business in one place

A system for business owners who aren't technical: orders, bookings, stock and invoices in one place, speaking each business's language.

Try the demo ↗ Start your free MIRA

Where it started

Where it came from

Many businesses run their day on notebooks, spreadsheets and chats. Each tool tells part of the story, and at the end of the day nobody has the whole of it.

And existing systems tend to speak the software's language, not the business's: a table is a "record" and a patient is a "customer".

The decisions

Why we did it this way

  1. One core for many businesses

    A coffee shop, a repair shop, a salon and a clinic share more than it seems. Modularity has a single core that adapts to each one, turning on only what that business needs.

  2. Each business's own words

    A coffee shop talks about tables; a repair shop, about vehicles; a clinic, about patients. The system changes its words so every owner feels at home.

  3. Made for people who aren't technical

    It's built for the owner who serves, charges and decides. If you need to read a manual to use it, something went wrong.

  4. The books aren't erased

    Billing keeps the full trail of every movement. What happened with the money stays written and isn't rewritten.

  5. Tested before promised

    Every part of the system has automated tests, over 400 in the core, so what works today keeps working tomorrow.
