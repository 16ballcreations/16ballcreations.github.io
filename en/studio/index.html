The studio

Who's behind it

A small studio on purpose: you talk to the person who does the work, from start to finish.

A pool table holds fifteen balls. The sixteenth doesn't exist, so we invented it. In that same spirit we bring to life any idea that wants to tell a single story, one that's seen and recognized wherever people look.

01

Who we are

16 Ball Creations was born in 2013 with Renne Castellanos, its founder, who sums it up in one line: “I bring ideas to life.” More than fourteen years building systems for companies taught him to do it with order. From Medellín, Colombia, we've worked with brands in Colombia, Venezuela and Costa Rica, in Spanish and in English.

02

What we do

Your Brand Ecosystem: your brand essence, a brand that knows who it is and says it the same way everywhere, and your digital ecosystem, where people find you: your website, your store and your channels.

03

How we do it

First we listen: your MIRA and a call. Then we send you a written proposal, build with you watching and check everything before handing it over. No templates: every project is made to fit. And everything is in your name: we teach you how to run it, so you're in control of your business without depending on anyone, us included.

The rules of the table

Why pool?

Pool is a game of precision: every shot is worked out before it's taken. A brand is the same: every piece you publish says something, and they all have to say the same thing.

Read the table

Before every shot you read the table. Before designing, we understand your brand: where it comes from and where it's going.

Safety play

Playing safe means leaving nothing to chance. We think about what could go wrong so it doesn't happen to your customers.

Every shot counts

Every piece is a shot: the logo, the website, a post. We check everything before handing it over, so none of them is out of tune.
