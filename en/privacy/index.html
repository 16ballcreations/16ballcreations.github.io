Your data, in plain words

What we keep when you write to us or visit the site, why, who we share it with and how to ask us to correct or delete it.

Who is responsible for your data

16 Ball Creations, a brand studio in Medellín, Colombia, run by Renne Castellanos. For anything about your data, write to crea@16ballcreations.com. We handle your data under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013. This policy also covers the Modularity site, modularity.16ballcreations.com.

What you tell us

When you start your MIRA or use the contact form we keep your name, your brand's name, how to reach you and your answers. We use them to reply, prepare the call and, if we work together, the proposal. We don't sell them or use them to send you advertising you didn't ask for.

How the site is read

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is read: how long people stay on each page, how far they scroll and where they click. That tells us which parts make sense and which need work. Clarity also keeps a replay of the visit, with anything typed into forms hidden.

To link the pages of one visit, Clarity sets two first-party cookies (_clck, one year, and _clsk, one day). We don't use it for ads. Microsoft processes this data, which may leave Colombia; its policy is at privacy.microsoft.com.

  • If you visit from Europe, we ask first and nothing loads until you say yes.
  • From anywhere else it loads when you arrive, and a notice tells you so with the option to say no. If you say no, it turns off, its cookies are deleted and it won't load again.

Who else sees your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the forms database, and keeps the IP address and basic details of each visit in its logs.
  • Microsoft, through Clarity, while measurement is on.

How long we keep it

What you tell us in the forms, for as long as we stay in touch or until you ask us to delete it. In Clarity, Microsoft keeps replays for 30 days and figures and heatmaps for up to 13 months.

Your rights

You can access, correct, update and ask us to delete your data, or withdraw the consent you gave us. Write to crea@16ballcreations.com: we answer inquiries within 10 business days and complaints within 15. If you're not satisfied, you can go to Colombia's Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (SIC).
