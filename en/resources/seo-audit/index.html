SEO and AEO audit

Your website can be well made and still not show up. This audit tells you why, with the same method we use in the studio. The files are in Spanish; Claude can write the report in English.

What it does

It sees your website the way others do

01

The way Google sees it

It checks whether Google can find, read and understand every page: what blocks it, what's too much and what's missing.

02

The way AI sees it

It asks ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot about your business, and checks what your website needs so they name you when someone looks for what you do.

03

What to fix first

It gives you a 0 to 100 score, the evidence for every point and the fixes in order, starting with the ones that add the most.

How to use it

  1. 01

    Download it

    It's a zip with one folder, auditoria-seo.

  2. 02

    Install it in Claude

    On claude.ai, upload it as is in the skills section of the settings. In Claude Code, unzip it into the .claude/skills folder.

  3. 03

    Ask for the audit

    Write, for example: “Run an SEO audit of https://yoursite.com, report in English.” Claude will ask what kind of business it is and which accounts you can access.

It works best with an agent that can browse and run commands, like Claude Code. Without one, the checklist and the guide can also be followed by hand.

No promises nobody can keep

Nobody can promise you the top spot on Google, or more sales. This audit doesn't: it tells you, with evidence, what's working, what's holding your website back and in what order to fix it.
