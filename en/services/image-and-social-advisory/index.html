Who it's for

Sometimes you don't need to build anything new: you need to put things in order. We review how your brand looks and speaks everywhere it shows up, and tell you what to change so it all feels like one brand. It works for businesses and for personal brands.

What's included

  • A review of your profiles: photo, name, bio, highlights
  • What your brand says today, and what it should say
  • An image guide: colors, photos and style for your posts
  • Personal image advice, if you are the brand
  • A written plan with what to change first

How we do it

Step by step

Always in the same order: first we understand your brand, then we build. No step gets skipped.

  1. Your MIRA

    Five questions about your brand (about three minutes, free) and a 30-minute call. First we understand where your brand comes from, why it was born and where it's going.

  2. The review

    We go through your social profiles, your website and your pieces, and note where the story breaks.

  3. Your plan

    We give you, in writing, what to change, in what order and how, with examples.

  4. Follow-through

    If you want, we apply it with you, or we do it for you. We always show you how, so the control stays in your hands.

Questions

What people ask us

If your question isn't here, write to us: we reply within 24 hours.

How is this different from the MIRA?

The MIRA is the first, free look, to know where to start. The advisory is the full job: an in-depth review, a written plan and follow-through.

Does it work for a personal brand?

Yes. We've done it with artists and with outreach projects: putting profiles, image and voice in order so the person and the brand say the same thing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need; after the call we send you a written proposal, with no commitment. Your MIRA and the first call are free, and if you want the full diagnosis in writing, the brand audit costs US$200.

Do you work with clients outside Colombia?

Yes. We're based in Medellín, Colombia, and we work by video call with brands anywhere. We've done projects in Colombia, Venezuela and Costa Rica, and we work in English or Spanish.
