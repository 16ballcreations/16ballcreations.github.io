Who it's for

An online store is your brand serving someone who can't walk into your shop. So we build it with your voice and your style, not the face of a template: a catalog that's easy to browse and a simple way to order.

What's included

  • Catalog with photos, prices and filters
  • Orders through the site or straight to your WhatsApp
  • Product pages written in your brand's words
  • Looks right on phones, where most people shop
  • A simple place to update products and prices
  • Ready for Google: titles, descriptions and a sitemap

How we do it

Step by step

Always in the same order: first we understand your brand, then we build. No step gets skipped.

  1. Your MIRA

    Five questions about your brand (about three minutes, free) and a 30-minute call. First we understand where your brand comes from, why it was born and where it's going.

  2. Your catalog

    We organize your products: categories, photos, descriptions, and how your customers will browse them.

  3. Design and build

    We show you how it will look, build it, and test every order from start to finish.

  4. Up and running

    We check everything before we hand it over, show you how to use it, and stay by your side afterwards.

Questions

What people ask us

If your question isn't here, write to us: we reply within 24 hours.

Can I receive orders through WhatsApp?

Yes. It's the most common setup in our projects: customers put together their order in the store and it reaches your WhatsApp ready to go.

Who uploads the products?

We load the initial catalog and show you how to add, change and remove products.

How much does it cost?

Projects start at US$400. The final price depends on what you need; after the call we send you a written proposal, with no commitment. Your MIRA and the first call are free.

Do you work with clients outside Colombia?

Yes. We're based in Medellín, Colombia, and we work by video call with brands anywhere. We've done projects in Colombia, Venezuela and Costa Rica, and we work in English or Spanish.
