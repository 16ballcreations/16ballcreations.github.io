How we do it

Step by step

Always in the same order: first we understand your brand, then we build. No step gets skipped.

  1. Your MIRA

    Five questions about your brand (about three minutes, free) and a 30-minute call. First we understand where your brand comes from, why it was born and where it's going.

  2. The research

    We look your brand up the way a customer would: on Google, on Maps, on your social media, on other sites and in AI tools.

  3. The document

    We write every finding channel by channel, with its priority and the exact way to fix it.

  4. The walkthrough

    We go through it with you in 45 minutes by video call and answer your questions.

Questions

What people ask us

If your question isn't here, write to us: we reply within 24 hours.

How much does the audit cost?

US$200, paid upfront. If within the next 30 days you decide to have us make the corrections and your website, what you paid is credited to the project.

Does the audit include the corrections?

No. We tell you what to fix and how, so you or your team can do it. If you'd rather we do it, we quote it separately and the audit is credited.

How is it different from the MIRA?

The MIRA is free: five questions about your brand, a call and a first look. The audit is the full detail, in writing: what's failing, on which channel and how to fix it.

Can my business show up when someone asks ChatGPT?

Nobody can promise that an AI will name you. What we can do is give it something clear to understand: your name, what you do and where you are, said the same way on your website, your Google profile and your social media. Google and AI tools recommend businesses they recognize as one, and that's exactly what aligning your brand does.
