Who it's for

A brand doesn't start with a logo: it starts with why it exists. We begin with your story, who you're speaking to and what you want them to feel, and from there we shape everything people see. The result is a brand that's recognizable in every piece, from the sign on the door to your latest post.

What's included

  • Your story in writing: origin, purpose and who you speak to
  • Your logo in order, with its versions and uses
  • Colors and typefaces
  • Brand voice: how it speaks, and how it doesn't
  • Brand guidelines: how to use it well, so anyone can apply it without blurring it
  • If your logo needs a redesign, we do it separately with our partner designer

How we do it

Step by step

Always in the same order: first we understand your brand, then we build. No step gets skipped.

  1. Your MIRA

    Five questions about your brand (about three minutes, free) and a 30-minute call. First we understand where your brand comes from, why it was born and where it's going.

  2. Your story

    Together we write where the brand comes from and what it should make people feel. That story guides every decision.

  3. Your identity

    We put your logo, colors and voice in order, and refine them with you until they feel like yours.

  4. Your guidelines

    We hand over the files and the brand guidelines, so anyone can use your brand without blurring it.

Questions

What people ask us

If your question isn't here, write to us: we reply within 24 hours.

I already have a logo. Do I have to change it?

Not necessarily. Often the logo is fine and what's missing is order: colors that don't repeat, social profiles telling a different story. Your MIRA tells us whether to update it or just tidy things up.

What do I get at the end?

Your brand guidelines, express or complete, with your logo files in every format, the color palette, the typefaces and the voice, ready to use.

Does it work for a personal brand?

Yes. We've done it with artists and outreach projects: putting image and voice in order so the person and the brand say the same thing.

How much does it cost?

Everything has a fixed price, shown on each service: the brand audit costs US$150, brand guidelines go from US$390 to US$910, and websites come in three plans, from US$970 to US$2,580. Your MIRA and the first call are free.

Where do I start?

With your MIRA: five questions about your brand. We use your answers to prepare the call, and we write back within 24 hours to schedule it.
