Who it's for

As a business grows, notebooks and spreadsheets start telling different stories. We build custom systems so everything lives in one place, in your business's own words, and each person sees only what they need.

What's included

  • Orders and work orders
  • Inventory that warns you when something runs low
  • Calendar and appointments
  • Invoices and a record of every sale
  • Customers and their history
  • Permissions: each employee sees only their part

How we do it

Step by step

Always in the same order: first we understand your brand, then we build. No step gets skipped.

  1. Your MIRA

    Five questions about your brand (about three minutes, free) and a 30-minute call. First we understand where your brand comes from, why it was born and where it's going.

  2. How you work today

    We walk through how your business runs with you: what gets written down, where things get lost, what you want to see at a glance.

  3. Built in parts

    We build it module by module, starting with what hurts most, and you try it early on.

  4. Up and running

    We check everything before we hand it over, show you how to use it, and stay by your side afterwards.
