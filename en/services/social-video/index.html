How we do it

Step by step

Always in the same order: first we understand your brand, then we build. No step gets skipped.

  1. Your MIRA

    Five questions about your brand (about three minutes, free) and a 30-minute call. First we understand where your brand comes from, why it was born and where it's going.

  2. Your content line

    We define what your brand talks about, how it says it and how its videos look.

  3. Filming and editing

    We produce the videos and pieces, and review them with you before they go out.

Questions

What people ask us

If your question isn't here, write to us: we reply within 24 hours.

Do you promise more followers?

No. Our job is to make your content tell the same story as your brand, with quality and consistency. Followers depend on many things nobody fully controls.

Do I have to be on camera?

Not always. We decide together based on your brand: there are formats with a person, with the product, or with just a voice.

How much does it cost?

Projects start at US$200. The final price depends on what you need; after the call we send you a written proposal, with no commitment. Your MIRA and the first call are free.

Do you work with clients outside Colombia?

Yes. We're based in Medellín, Colombia, and we work by video call with brands anywhere. We've done projects in Colombia, Venezuela and Costa Rica, and we work in English or Spanish.
